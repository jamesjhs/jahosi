# Official adult social care resources

Source list reviewed for SocialQA: 20 September 2026.

SocialQA should answer adult social care funding and care-navigation questions for England from official, statutory, NHS, regulator, ombudsman, recognised guidance and official statistics sources only. It must not give legal, financial, medical, safeguarding, provider-selection or complaint-handling advice. It should signpost the relevant official route and tell users to check local details before acting.
