TeachMe policy, use policy and disclaimer

How TeachMe works, what sources it may use, how information is handled, and what parents should check before relying on any answer.

What TeachMe Is

TeachMe is an experimental AI-assisted information page for parents and carers navigating the English early years and primary National Curriculum. It is not a substitute for a child's school, teacher, special educational needs assessment, safeguarding advice, legal advice or professional education advice.

TeachMe is designed to explain curriculum ideas in plain English, adapt answers to Reception through Year 6, and suggest practical home support that stays realistic for families.

Source Boundary

TeachMe's answers should be grounded in the resources listed in resources.md. If a user asks for a website, worksheet, video, tutor, app, publisher or organisation outside that file, TeachMe may signpost to it with an appropriate link, but should clearly label it as outside scope and should not use it as evidence for the answer.

Outside-scope links should be neutral signposts, not endorsements, rankings, reviews or recommendations. Where TeachMe is not confident of the exact link, it should name the organisation or official site to check rather than inventing a URL.

The source list was last reviewed on 18 September 2026. Source pages can change. Always open the original GOV.UK, EEF or listed source before relying on exact wording, dates, assessment details or policy requirements.

Use Limits

  • TeachMe can make mistakes, omit context or phrase source material too strongly.
  • Answers are general and cannot know your child's school, class, teacher, needs, assessments or circumstances.
  • For concerns about progress, attendance, anxiety, special educational needs, safeguarding or wellbeing, speak to the school or an appropriate qualified professional.
  • Do not enter confidential school records, full names, addresses, medical details or other sensitive personal information into the chat.

Privacy

The chat sends the question, selected school year, selected source context and recent temporary browser-session chat history to the TeachMe server so an answer can be generated. Chat history is stored in browser session storage on the user's device for the current browser session. Display preferences may be stored in local storage.

TeachMe does not provide user accounts and is not designed to build profiles, target advertising or sell user data. Hosting, security, anti-abuse and upstream AI services may retain technical metadata according to their own operational settings.

The data controller is James Rowson. For privacy questions, correction requests or communication preferences, use the Jahosi contact page.

Parent Responsibility

By using TeachMe, you choose to ask questions of your own accord. You remain responsible for checking the original source, deciding whether the answer fits your child, and discussing individual concerns with the school.
