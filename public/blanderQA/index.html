Plain-English glossary

EEVEE

Blender's real-time renderer. Useful for quick look development and previewing a scene while you work.

Cycles

Blender's path-tracing renderer. Useful when you want physically based lighting and final output.

Principled BSDF

The main Blender 4.0 shader node for building materials with a broadly standard workflow.

Geometry Nodes

A procedural system for building and modifying geometry with node networks instead of only direct mesh edits.

Modifiers

Non-destructive object or mesh operations that let you keep options open until you apply them.

Constraints

Rules that control movement, rotation or relationships between objects or bones.

Drivers

Links that make one property respond to another property or control input.

Compositor

Blender's node-based post-processing space for combining, correcting or enhancing rendered images.
