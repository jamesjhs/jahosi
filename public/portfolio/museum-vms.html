Museum Volunteer Management System

A TypeScript/Next.js, React, Tailwind and Prisma (database)-powered website designed to make my life easier for Volunteer Coordinators at Museums or Volunteer-led organisations, this project was borne of the simple need to organise members more effectively. I had the simple self-defined brief: create a method by which staff, trustees, team leaders and volunteers could be structured and managed under one site.

The goal is integration with current Sharepoint/Azure webservers, but this proof-of-concept model has been created iteratively over many days to encompass the 'barebones' of the system to hopefully drive the museum into the future. This project would have been impossible to do in such a short period of time without the offerings of GitHub/Copilot!

Problem and goal

Volunteer-led organisations often need to coordinate people with different roles, responsibilities, availability and permissions, but they may not have a purpose-built system for that work. The goal was to sketch a practical platform for managing staff, trustees, team leaders and volunteers in one place.

Solution and progress

The current version is a proof of concept rather than a finished production deployment. It demonstrates the structure and interface needed for member organisation, and it gives the museum a concrete future direction for a more integrated SharePoint/Azure-aware volunteer system.

Implementation

The prototype uses a modern TypeScript stack with React, Tailwind and Prisma. The implementation focus is data modelling and role structure: prove that the organisation can be represented clearly first, then refine integration, permissions and operational workflows.
