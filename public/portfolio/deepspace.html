Project

Deep Space Command

Original space exploration roleplay · diplomacy and philosophy · animated starfield

Deep Space Command is a creative conversation app for exploring difficult choices through the eyes of a seasoned commander of a long-range exploratory vessel. Bring a first-contact dilemma, an archaeological discovery or a crisis in an unfamiliar star system, and develop the story through conversation.

The commander is instructed to favour diplomacy, ethical debate and peaceful resolution, drawing on classical literature, history and archaeology to frame new problems. Before making a final decision, the commander seeks the expertise of specialized senior staff. Ships, crew and backstories are created for an original universe.

Why build it?

This project explores how a deliberately defined point of view can shape an AI conversation. Its purpose is imaginative storytelling and thoughtful discussion: considering competing responsibilities, listening to different perspectives and asking what a peaceful outcome might require.

How it works

The app runs inside the Jahosi Node.js and Express backend, using the same server-side AI connection, request limits and Turnstile session checks as the Q&A apps. Its commander instructions stay on the server, and only a bounded conversation history is sent with each message.

The interface uses an HTML5 canvas starfield over a nebula-style background. Animation can be paused and respects reduced-motion preferences. Conversation history stays in memory in the current tab and is cleared on reload or when a new conversation is started.
